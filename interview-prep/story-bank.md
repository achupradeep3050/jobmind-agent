| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Situation | Manual deployment processes taking 3+ hours |

## Tips
- Use the STAR+R format for all behavioral questions
- Each reflection signals seniority — show what you learned, not just what happened
- Keep 5-10 master stories that adapt to multiple question angles
- Quantify everything: time saved, money saved, incidents resolved
